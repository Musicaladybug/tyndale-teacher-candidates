The Closed Circuit TVs around the School of Education are a great way to
celebrate the work our teacher candidates are doing — and we'd love to feature
you.

## What to submit

Photos of yourself and your learning: classroom moments, practicum highlights,
group projects, or anything that captures your growth as an educator. Candid,
real, and people-centric works best.

## A few tips

1. Make sure everyone in the photo is comfortable being featured.
2. Higher-resolution images look best on the big screens.
3. Add a sentence of context so viewers know what they're seeing.

Head to the **TCs on TV** card on the home page to submit a photo. We can't wait
to see what you share.
