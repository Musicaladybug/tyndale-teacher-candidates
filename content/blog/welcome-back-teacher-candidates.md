Welcome to a new term! Whether you're just starting your journey as a teacher
candidate or returning for another year, this hub is your home base for
everything School of Education.

## What you'll find here

- **Key links** — the pages you'll come back to most: schedules, section
  lookups, course locations, and more.
- **The blog** — stories, updates, and advice from faculty, staff, and fellow
  candidates (you're reading it now).
- **Who to contact** — never wonder who handles what again.

## Your first week

Take ten minutes to bookmark this page and skim the key links. Find your
section, check where your courses are meeting, and say hi to the people in your
cohort. Small steps now make a big difference later.

We're glad you're here.
