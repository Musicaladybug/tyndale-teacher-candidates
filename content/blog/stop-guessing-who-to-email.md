Picture this: it's 9:52pm, your assignment is due at midnight, and Moodle just
ate your submission. You have a question. You also have, somehow, four different
email addresses you think might be the right one — a name someone mentioned in
orientation, an address you half-remember from a course outline, a "reply-all"
from your first week that a friend forwarded you "just in case." You pick one,
send it into the void, and go to bed no closer to an answer.

Here's the thing: even if you'd picked right, nobody on our team is going to be
at their desk at 9:52pm. And that's exactly the point — the fix isn't a faster
reply at midnight, it's knowing, the moment you sit down the next morning,
exactly who to walk up to or email first. No more guessing the night before. No
more losing an hour the next day just tracking down the right person.

If that scene feels familiar, you're not alone — and it's exactly the problem we
set out to fix.

## The maze behind the classroom door

Every School of Education program runs on people, not just courses: someone
tracks your practicum placement, someone keeps your semester schedule straight,
someone signs off on your AQ paperwork, someone just wants to know how your
program experience is actually going. The trouble is, from the outside, that
team can look like a wall of job titles and closed office doors. Teacher
candidates end up doing the one thing nobody should have to do: guessing.

So we stopped making you guess.

## Introducing: Who to Contact

We've put together a single, one-page guide — [Who to Contact](/who-to-contact)
— that lays out exactly who to reach for exactly what, right down to the office
number. No more
forwarded email chains. No more "I think it's the Coordinator? Or maybe the
Officer?" No more standing in the A Wing hallway reading name plates like they're
going to reveal a secret passage.

Here's a taste of who's on the other side of that door:

- **Candice**, our Education Programs Officer, is your first call for Weekly
  Announcements, Moodle troubleshooting, and Chromebook loans.
- **Jaclyn**, Education Programs Coordinator, keeps classroom technology running
  — and if you've heard whispers about quilts, chapel, or the Bible course,
  that's her too.
- **Anthony**, our Practicum Coordinator, is the one to ask about placement
  questions, practicum forms, and VSS.
- **Blair**, Practicum Team Lead and Associate Professor, is your contact for
  practicum-related concerns and anything involving your Faculty Advisor.
- **Mariam**, Manager of Education Programs, keeps semester schedules, course
  registration, and OCT certification questions on track.
- **Catherine**, Administrator of Education Programs, handles AQs and ABQs,
  program handbooks and policies, and funded grant projects.
- **Heather**, our Dean, wants to hear about the big picture — your overall
  experience in the program, concerns that touch both cohorts, and where
  Graduate School might take you next.

Seven people. Seven very different reasons to knock on a door. One page that
tells you which one.

## Come find us

We mean that literally. While you're always welcome to email, we'd genuinely
love for you to drop by — our offices are on Level 300, A Wing. Grab the guide,
stop by, put a face to the name. That's not just faster than email; it's the
whole point. This is a small program in the best sense of the word: we know your
names, and now, you'll know ours.

The [Who to Contact guide](/who-to-contact) is available now — keep it
bookmarked, keep it [printed](/who-to-contact/print), keep it wherever you'll
actually look for it the next time a question hits at 9:52pm. You won't get an answer that night, but come morning, you won't waste a
minute figuring out who to ask. You'll just know.
